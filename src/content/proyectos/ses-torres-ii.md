Nada brillante: en la isla, el brillo acusa la salinidad y el polvo en una temporada."
    - Los dormitorios se reparten entre las dos plantas. El principal se resuelve en suite, con una pared de piedra natural tras el cabecero y un vestidor pasante que hace de esclusa hasta el baño, de doble seno. Es una secuencia que da intimidad sin necesidad de pasillos.
    - "Arriba, la azotea funciona como una habitación más: solárium, piscina de arista y una celosía de madera que corta el sol alto sin cerrar la vista. Y abajo, en el sótano, el hormigón se dejó visto. Es la planta que nadie fotografía y la que mejor enseña cómo está hecha una casa."
  en:
    - "The ground floor reads at a glance. Living room, kitchen and dining room share one continuous ceiling, and the floor-to-ceiling joinery slides until it disappears into the wall: when it is open, the terrace stops being outside. It is the design decision that orders the whole house."
    - "The materials are few and they return again and again. Oak on the kitchen fronts, the wardrobes and the vertical battens that accompany the stair. Large-format dark stone on the worktops. Matt white on the walls, no skirting, junctions resolved flush. Nothing glossy: on the island, gloss shows up salt and dust within a single season."
    - The bedrooms are split across both floors. The main one is en suite, with a natural stone wall behind the headboard and a walk-through dressing area acting as an airlock to the double-basin bathroom. It is a sequence that gives privacy without needing corridors.
    - "Upstairs, the roof works as one more room: sun deck, sharp-edged pool and a timber screen that cuts the high sun without closing off the view. And below, in the basement, the concrete was left exposed. It is the floor nobody photographs, and the one that best shows how a house is made."
