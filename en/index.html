Frequently Asked Questions

We answer your questions about construction in Ibiza

How much does it cost to build a luxury home in Ibiza?

The cost of building a luxury villa in Ibiza depends on multiple factors: location, size, finishes and design complexity. As a luxury construction specialist, our projects typically start from €3,000/m² with premium finishes. A 300m² villa with infinity pool, home automation and high-end materials can range between €900,000 and €1,500,000. We offer personalized quotes without obligation.

How long does it take to build a villa with pool in Ibiza?

The average timeframe to build a complete luxury home in Ibiza is 12-18 months, including:

  • Project and permits: 2-4 months
  • Structure construction: 6-8 months
  • Finishes and interior design: 4-6 months

As a turnkey construction company, we handle the entire process so you don't have to worry about anything.

What does the turnkey construction service include?

Our turnkey service for luxury properties in Ibiza includes:

  • Complete architectural project and 3D renders
  • Management of municipal licenses and permits
  • Construction with continuous supervision
  • Interior design and premium material selection
  • Infinity pool installation with filtration system
  • Gardens with native Mediterranean vegetation
  • Home automation, climate control and security systems
  • 10-year structural guarantee

Why choose a local Ibiza construction company?

Choosing a construction company in Ibiza with local experience is essential because:

  • We know the specific island urban planning regulations
  • We design respecting typical Mediterranean architectural style
  • We work with trusted local suppliers (better price and quality)
  • We understand the climate and use materials resistant to salinity
  • Agile permit management with the Island Council

With over 15 years on the island, we are your best choice for building in Ibiza.

Can I fully customize the design of my villa?

Absolutely! Every luxury home we build is unique and 100% customized. You will work directly with our team of architects and interior designers to:

  • Choose the exact layout you need
  • Select premium materials (marble, wood, natural stone)
  • Design your dream pool (infinity, overflow, heated)
  • Integrate custom smart home technology
  • Create unique outdoor spaces (chill-out, BBQ, outdoor gym)

Your villa will be exactly as you imagine it, without compromises.
